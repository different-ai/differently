# Code of conduct

Differently adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome
newcomers, and keep discussion focused on the work.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/different-ai/differently/security/advisories/new)
marked "conduct", or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled
confidentially, and maintainers who are involved in a report recuse themselves.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
